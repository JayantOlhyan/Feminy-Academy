## Main finding

The reference is a complete selling sequence, not just a visual theme: explain the offer, show examples, make the process understandable, demonstrate the product, present evidence, handle comparisons and prerequisites, establish the creator's credibility, repeat the offer, then answer objections.

Use this sequence as a design reference for Feminy while retaining its light palette, genuine course pricing schedule, own crest and real academy material. The reference's lime, black panels, software promises and purchase mechanics are not Feminy's business facts.

## 2. Visual system

- Warm, light neutral main background; white cards; lavender secondary panels; strong lime accent. Some large panels are dark. Feminy should keep ivory, white, lilac and pale gold with its own magenta/gold accents, as explicitly requested.
- Narrow centred reading column, with the main example rail extending much wider. Larger sections often alternate between centred introduction and split visual/text layout.
- Bold sans-serif headlines, grey supporting copy, tiny utility captions and occasional handwritten notes. The design relies on clear changes in type size rather than many different typefaces.
- Rounded media, panels and pill buttons; soft shadows distinguish selected larger surfaces. Consistent inset spacing makes product mockups feel structured.
- Rhythm comes from changing section form: wide rail, three steps, one large showcase, split walkthrough, evidence mosaic, comparison, prerequisites, portrait/story, final offer, accordion.
- Spacing is generous between major arguments and tighter inside a single argument. Do not give every small element its own card.
- The PDF is a desktop-style composition. It does not prove the mobile layout, touch behaviour, keyboard support or reduced-motion behaviour.

## 6. Hover, touch and motion acceptance

- Desktop: small hover lift/scale, clear play affordance, optional muted preview, stop background rail motion while interacting. Maintain visible keyboard focus.
- Mobile: tap opens, swipe browses, touching pauses, arrows remain usable. No function may depend on hover.
- Infinite rails must wrap without blank space, reset focus correctly and expose each unique item only once to assistive technology.
- Reduced motion disables automatic scrolling and ornamental movement. No audible autoplay.
- Rotate text only within a reserved space; no layout jumps or collisions like those visible in the PDF capture.
- Large mixed evidence grids must reflow in meaningful reading order; message screenshots must be enlargeable and not become illegible thumbnails.
- Compare options in stacked cards or an intentionally scrollable region on narrow screens. Never force a desktop-wide comparison onto a phone.
- Countdown keeps one global deadline and actual price consequence. Include the date/time, not only a ticking number.
